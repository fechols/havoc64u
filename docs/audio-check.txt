H.A.V.O.C. - Ultimate audio and combat frame-rate comparison

The new build is in D:\havoc. The previous v15 build is retained separately
in D:\havoc-audio-baseline. Keep each folder's PRG, REU and CFG together.

Use the same display, video standard, joystick and audio output for both runs.
Import havoc.cfg and check that CPU Speed is 64. The supplied configurations
are identical: four UltiSIDs, Left 1 / Right 1 panning, and the same mixer gains.
Leave the joystick's hardware autofire disabled.

For each build:
1. Reset, load that folder's havoc.reu at REU address zero, and run its havoc.prg.
2. Start a fresh game and press F to show the frame-rate readout. Let the stage
   music change finish before measuring combat.
3. Play the same combat section for at least 30 seconds, holding fire. Follow
   the same route and use bombs at the same points: double-tap fire within
   half a second. Include a section with many hits and overlapping explosions.
4. Note the normal FPS range and the lowest readings during sustained combat
   and bomb explosions. Record deaths, stage changes and music transitions
   separately. Repeat three times per build, alternating old/new order.

Listen for punch and clarity: a deep, snappy "pew" cannon that no longer
blurs under sustained fire, hull impacts as a heavy thud and crunch (not a
ricochet), deep ship explosions that hit at full chip volume for four
frames, re-blast once and sink into a low rumble, and a bomb that hits at
full chip volume for three frames before settling. The barrel roll should be
a rising whoosh with a spinning tone, not a wobble.
Check that the brief full-volume punches on hits, explosions and the bomb
sit well against the music and do not overwhelm it; the sustained level is
unchanged. Filter sweeps and effect durations still use 50 Hz timing. Check
left/right placement.

The F readout averages frames over roughly one second; it cannot identify
every individual frame stall. Emulator SFX benchmarks and native DAC models
are separate measurements. This check includes real rendering, DAC music,
SID effects and the physical Ultimate together; it is the final check for
the sustained-combat slowdown reported on the machine.
