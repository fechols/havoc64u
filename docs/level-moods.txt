H.A.V.O.C. - LEVEL MOODS

A creative brief for music production.

----------

1. Lowland Approach

Open, adventurous, and optimistic: green hills and a sense of setting out on
something big. The music should give the player confidence and the pleasure
of flight, with an easy forward drive that leaves room for the campaign's
later intensity to grow.


2. Crystal Cave

Luminous, mysterious, and full of discovery, as though flying into a
beautiful place whose secrets are still hidden. The enclosed passage adds
alertness and a little unease beneath the wonder. Keep a feeling of motion
through the cave, with enough space for its atmosphere to register.


3. Iron Canyon

Driving, focused, and increasingly tense, with the canyon walls giving the
flight a feeling of confinement and speed. The player is pressing on toward
the first major confrontation. Build determination and anticipation here,
saving the strongest sense of conflict for Canyon Gate.


4. Canyon Gate

The Gorge Warden is the first boss, turning the canyon journey into a direct
confrontation. Begin with the pressure of approaching a defended obstacle,
then let the battle feel forceful and urgent. This is the first major
payoff: a hard-won breakthrough and a brief release afterward.


5. Ashen Wastes

Bleak, exposed, and determined, with the landscape making the journey feel
harsher after the first victory. There is room for a little weariness or
sadness, but the player is still pushing forward through combat. A
persistent pulse can carry that resolve into the next stretch.


6. Foundry Belt

Heavy, industrial, and relentless, suggesting the weight and repetition of
machinery all around the player. The surroundings should feel busy and
imposing, with a firm sense of forward motion. This section deepens the
pressure as the route continues toward the fortress and its core.


7. Fortress Run

Purposeful, martial, and increasingly urgent: the player is committed to the
assault and closing on a major target. Give the music a strong sense of
resolve under pressure. This is the buildup to Citadel Core, with tension
gathering as the next boss encounter approaches.


8. Citadel Core

Mechanical menace and concentrated pressure, as the assault reaches the
Citadel Heart boss. The approach should feel ominous, then tighten into a
forceful confrontation with a powerful machine. A sense of a heavy heartbeat
suits the battle, followed by relief when its defenses finally fall.


9. Storm Front

Turbulent, urgent, and exhilarating, with the feeling of being swept into
rough conditions after the Citadel victory. The music can surge and heave
while keeping the player moving. This starts a fresh rise in intensity as
the journey continues toward the carrier confrontation.


10. Meteor Reach

Restless, exposed, and unpredictable, carrying the storm section's momentum
into another hazardous stretch. The player should feel alert to trouble
ahead, with excitement and unease sharing the same space. Keep the energy
moving and unsettled as the route draws closer to Carrier Coast.


11. Carrier Coast

A looming threat comes into focus, with determination growing alongside the
tension. This is the immediate approach to Carrier Assault, so the music
should suggest a large confrontation getting closer. Let the player feel
committed and capable while holding back the full battle intensity.


12. Carrier Assault

A powerful, hard-fought confrontation with the Carrier boss and the climax
of the campaign's terrestrial stretch. Build from the approach into an
urgent battle that feels like a major achievement to win. Afterward, the
ascent into space brings release, exhilaration, and a new sense of scale.


13. Asteroid Belt

Fast, agile, and thrilling: reaching space opens up the view while debris
keeps the player busy. Give this level a lively sense of weaving and
dodging, with the excitement of entering a much larger arena. The mood is
adventurous, energized by the danger of the flight itself.


14. Shattered Moon

Vast, beautiful, and slightly melancholy, inviting a sense of awe at the
lunar scenery. Broader, more spacious movement can give this level a
reflective character after the Asteroid Belt's rush. Combat continues, so
preserve an underlying drive even as the emotional pace opens out.


15. Ship Graveyard

Mysterious, mournful, and uneasy, with abandoned hulls suggesting loss and
unanswered questions. The music should leave room for the player to take in
the wreckage and feel the loneliness of the setting. Keep tension
underneath: this is still an active combat area, with danger among the
remains.


16. Orbital Shipyard

Metallic, purposeful, and imposing, bringing renewed industrial energy after
the graveyard's unease. A precise, insistent drive suits the structures and
machinery of the shipyard. This approach to the Dreadnought builds pressure
ahead of the campaign's final battles.


17. Space Carrier

Massive, threatening, and combative, with the Dreadnought giving the
approach a sense of overwhelming scale. Let anticipation develop into the
force of the penultimate boss battle, with determination answering the
threat. Its defeat earns a breath of relief, while the final challenge still
lies ahead.


18. Jump Gate

Begin with awe, mystery, and the promise of reaching a monumental
destination. The Gate Guardian turns that anticipation into the final boss
confrontation: urgent, determined, and carrying the weight of the whole
journey.
